About

Systems-first approach to cybersecurity. SOC operations, incident response, and infrastructure hardening built on operational experience and deep technical knowledge.

I work at the systems level - not because it's trendy, but because it's the only approach that actually solves problems.

I've built and operated complete business systems from the ground up - network infrastructure, operational security, vendor relations, payroll systems, and client delivery under real-world constraints. I've wired networks, managed critical operations, and kept systems running when failure wasn't an option. That operational experience taught me how systems degrade, how users behave under pressure, and how small architectural decisions cascade into failures that break everything downstream.

I didn't start learning technology recently. I started at age 2, learning DOS commands before I could read. By 13, I was building custom PCs with Bat Mitzvah money. In high school, I was customizing Blogspot sites with hand-coded HTML and CSS. That work eventually became a popular vegan cooking blog and led to a published cookbook. In 2009, I learned mobile device repair during the early days of smartphone retail. I've been troubleshooting, rebuilding, and reclaiming broken systems ever since.

In 2024, I enrolled at the California Institute of Applied Technology to formalize that applied systems knowledge into a structured cybersecurity credential. I completed CompTIA A+, Network+, and Security+ in under eight weeks while maintaining a 4.0 GPA, then completed my A.A.S. in Computer Information Systems, Cybersecurity, Summa Cum Laude, in July 2026. Current work includes Red Siege coursework in penetration testing and Kerberos, Cisco Ethical Hacker, CompTIA CyberDefense Pro and AI Prompting Essentials, and hands-on training across offensive security, malware analysis, and cloud infrastructure.

I approach security the same way I've always approached operations: by understanding constraints, lifecycles, and the interactions between layers. A secure system isn't built by adding tools. It's built by designing for degradation, recovery, and human behavior under pressure.

My current focus includes SOC monitoring and analysis, incident response, endpoint hardening, Identity & Access Management, cloud security architecture, AI-supported defense strategies, and human risk awareness. These areas evolve as the threat landscape shifts - I adapt methodology to meet operational reality, not the other way around.

Recent work includes shipping Hayabusa MCP v0.1.0 for controlled EVTX analysis and building a hardened Fedora-based agentic security harness for CyberStrike. I validated the harness through controlled live-model execution, constrained Hayabusa MCP orchestration, and a separate end-to-end offline forensic investigation with evidence provenance, payload containment, and deterministic result validation.

I also conceived and co-founded CIAT's weekly Beginners CTF, which I co-lead with a CEH instructor. I teach sessions independently when needed, most recently guiding participants through TryHackMe's ContAInment AI-security challenge.

Outside of my own work, I co-run Crumpled Thoughts, a nonprofit that provides literacy and technology education to underrepresented communities. I teach cybersecurity fundamentals, digital safety, and technical problem-solving. My wife teaches literacy and critical thinking. We work with people from all backgrounds (POC, LGBTQ+, immigrants, and low-income communities) to build the skills needed to navigate modern life with confidence.

My applied bachelor's degree in Computer Information Systems begins in September 2026. I continue independent coursework and project work alongside it, and I am open to opportunities that value systems thinking, operational rigor, and the ability to solve problems that do not have documented solutions yet.
