About

Offensive security across applications, systems, networks, and AI.

I have spent most of my life taking computers apart, rebuilding them, and trying to understand why they behave the way they do. Security gave that habit a useful place to go.

My work now spans web exploitation, Active Directory, reverse engineering, AI/ML security, and the systems connecting them. I came to it with years of operational work behind me: wiring networks, running web platforms, managing endpoint security, coordinating vendors, handling payroll systems, and keeping client work moving when something failed. That history taught me to look past the intended workflow and pay attention to what people and systems do under pressure.

I learned DOS commands at age two, before I could read. By 13, I was building custom PCs with Bat Mitzvah money. In high school, I customized Blogspot sites with hand-coded HTML and CSS. That work grew into a popular vegan cooking blog and led to a published cookbook. In 2009, I learned mobile device repair during the early days of smartphone retail. I've been troubleshooting, rebuilding, and reclaiming broken systems ever since.

In 2024, I enrolled at CIAT to put structure around the technical work I had already been doing. I completed the A.A.S. in Computer Information Systems, Cybersecurity in July 2026 with a 4.0 GPA. I begin the bachelor's program in late September. I earned CompTIA A+, Network+, and Security+ in under eight weeks, followed by ISC2 CC.

Outside school, I keep working through labs, CTFs, workshops, and projects in my home lab. Recent work includes web exploitation, Active Directory attack chains, reverse engineering, adversarial AI, and building an MCP server around Hayabusa in an isolated Proxmox VM. I am ranked in the top 1% on TryHackMe and serve as vice president of CIAT's Cybersecurity Club.

I also co-run Crumpled Thoughts, a nonprofit now established in New Mexico and Virginia. My role currently centers on oversight for its literacy, technology education, cybersecurity, and digital-safety programs. My wife leads its literacy and critical-thinking work.

I am looking for offensive security work involving web applications, identity, infrastructure, reverse engineering, or AI systems. I do my best work when a problem is unfamiliar, the documentation is incomplete, and the evidence has to decide what happens next.
