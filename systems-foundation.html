Repair, repurpose, verify

I have worked on hardware with missing documentation, abandoned drivers, and proprietary connectors. I have installed operating systems the manufacturers said had no chance of running on it. I start with what still works, identify the problem, and work my way through it one layer at a time.

I prefer repair and repurposing when that is the technically sound decision. Sometimes the answer is a rebuild. Sometimes it is a firmware change, a different operating system, or an adapter I guarantee I already own. Other times, the machine has reached the end of its useful life. I let the evidence decide.
