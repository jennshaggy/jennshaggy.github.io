Systems Archive (Historical & Experimental)#

This page captures long-arc systems work, experimental builds, and legacy technology projects that inform current infrastructure, security, and troubleshooting skillsets.

Note on archival evidence:
I maintain organized hard-copy photographic archives of my earliest systems builds. My first full computer build was completed at age 13, funded in part by Bat Mitzvah monetary gifts. Additional early hardware, networking, and repair work is documented in printed photographs and physical records.

Philosophy#

These systems are not curiosities.
They represent iterative learning, reverse-engineering instincts, and comfort operating without documentation — traits that directly translate to security analysis, incident response, and systems defense.
